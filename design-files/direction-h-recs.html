The reading order is the system: every row is numbered, every row has a one-line "why" in mono next to the headline, and every row has a "visa fler →" exit. Mystery rows are forbidden. The quick-rate banner at the top is the only persistent inline editing affordance — the user can refuse a row entirely, or correct one rating, and watch the cascade re-order on the next visit.

Duotone does the genre work silently: a glance at row 04 (svensk drama) is six terracotta tiles — visually unified, the genre is encoded in the palette. Row 05 (small-town atmosphere) shifts to plum. The chrome never has to label "drama" because the color does it.

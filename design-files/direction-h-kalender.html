BINGE · Kalender · v12 expanded screen 02 / 06

The week is the home, the calendar is the room.

Every page in Binge wears the same 7-day strip up top. The Kalender page is what happens when that strip opens up into a full-height board — each day a column, each episode a card, today a saffron wash, tonight highlighted in a soft saffron rule. Below the week, a month-of-context strip with week numbers in the left rail so users can scroll forward or back to plan around premieres and finales.

This is the page that proves the principle. Swedish viewers live by ISO weeks (v12); Binge honors that as the navigation unit instead of borrowing the iCal month grid. The month is reachable, never primary.

Read the page top-down: the standard week strip up top (the chrome's heartbeat); below it, the same week zoomed to full height as a board — each day a column, each tracked episode a small card with a duotone poster strip and time. Today's column carries the saffron wash; tonight's episode wears an inset 3px saffron rule.

Then the month context: a five-week grid with ISO numbers in the left rail. Episodes are tiny 14×4px pills, color-coded by the same duotone tones — so a glance at the month tells you "the next premiere is v13, the next finale is v14." Active week is highlighted in saffron. Click any week to load it above.

The legend at the bottom of the month strip is a quiet reveal of the system itself: the duotone tones are the genres, the saffron is "right now." Nothing else uses color.

BINGE · Mobile shell — three screens screen 05 / 06

The mobile design, with one deliberate inversion.

Mobile keeps everything that makes Binge Binge — the week strip up top, the duotone posters, the single saffron accent, big tabular numbers, mono captions. The deliberate inversion: the center slot of the bottom bar is Sök, not "Home." The most common mobile act is "is this on a Swedish streamer?" — and the center thumb-zone button should answer instantly.

Below, three phone screens: Hem, Bibliotek, Tillsammans. Same chrome, three different bodies. The top bar collapses to a single-line strip with the week number and seven day cells; everything else lives in the canvas.

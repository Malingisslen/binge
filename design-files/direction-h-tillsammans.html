The ballot, live in four people's living rooms.

A Tillsammans session is a short-lived shared screen — four phones or laptops looking at the same candidate list at the same time. The product's job: show presence, show votes the moment they arrive, show vetoes plainly, and let the host commit a final pick. Real-time without theatrics; dots, not animations.

How the room reads in one glance: presence bar at the top tells you who's there; the configuration ribbon below tells you what filters are in play (and shows that Johan has spent his veto). Then each candidate is a row with three things only — a duotone poster, a sentence of reasoning, and a tally of dots. Filled dots are yeses, empty dots are waiting, dashed dots are unjoined, and a saffron-deep dot is a veto.

The vetoed title stays visible: struck through, opacity 50%, with the vetoer and time stamped in saffron — never hidden, so the room understands why a title is gone. Saffron has one meaning across the entire product: now / live / decisive.

The resolved bar: when a candidate has unanimous yes, the host (and only the host) sees the ink bar at the bottom: "X has enad röst — välj nu." It's the only ink CTA on the page. Everyone else watches it appear in their feed.

Direction H — Schemat · final

A Swedish media tracker, organised around the week.

Single sans (Albert), single mono (JetBrains), single saffron accent, off-white paper, hairline rules, big tabular numbers. The week strip lives at the top of every page. Real TMDB posters arrive but pass through a per-title duotone treatment so the palette stays calm. Real content (episode stills, trailer thumbs, cast portraits, gallery) is shown raw, because those are previews, not navigation.

01

The week is the spine.

A persistent 7-day strip at the top of every page. Today wears a saffron wash; tonight wears a 2px saffron rule. Swedish viewers live by ISO weeks (v12); the system honors that as the navigation unit.

02

One accent does one job.

Saffron means now / live / decisive. It marks today, tonight's episode, the advisor's verdict, and the veto. Nothing else. The chrome and the palette are kept architecturally apart.

03

Posters bend to the palette.

Eight muted duotone tones — terracotta, slate, moss, clay, plum, steel, olive, oxblood — applied per title via SVG filter. Real TMDB bitmaps remain recognisable. The library looks like a curated collection.

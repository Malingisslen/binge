## 3. Rejected, and why

- **10 files over the 400-line threshold** (`WatchlistPage.tsx` at 1006, `groups.ts` at 998,
  `AuthContext.tsx` at 780, and seven more). `hygiene.fileSizeLines: 400` is declared in
  `shared-plugin.json`, but it is not one of the `hygiene.checks` entries, there is no
  accepted-large-files convention in this repo, and `CLAUDE.md` states no file-size rule.
  Filing a bulk split ticket would be inventing a rule rather than enforcing one. Recorded
  here as an observation instead.
- **`npm test` / `npm run lint` were deliberately not run.** With another session's work
  staged in the tree, a failure could not be attributed to committed code, and filing a
  ticket on someone's half-written change would be wrong. `npm run typecheck` was run and
  passed, which at least confirms the tree compiles.
- **App Check failing open without a site key** — already tracked as BIN-361, ops-gated.
- **`collectUserDataSnapshots`' unbounded groups query** — deliberately unlimited for GDPR
  completeness, unlike the four sites BIN-558 covers. Correct as written.
- **MOTN vendor-quota controls** — scanned thoroughly: transactional reservations,
  two-consecutive-run 429 confirmation, documented arithmetic. No bypass found. The one
  known gap is already BIN-541.
- **`mediaTypeDocId` client/functions drift** — checked, byte-identical.
